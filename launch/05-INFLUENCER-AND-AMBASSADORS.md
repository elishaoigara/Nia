# Nia — Creator & Ambassador Program

Principle: at launch budget, **nano/micro creators beat celebrities.** 30 creators with 10–80K engaged followers in Kenya, Nigeria, Ghana outperform one 2M macro. Pay partly in performance; reward with status (Founding badges, verified-in-Circle flair) as much as cash.

## 1. Creator tiers & rates (indicative, USD)

| Tier | Followers | Deliverable | Fee | Performance bonus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nano | 2–10K | 1 video + 1 story/status | $20–50 + Founding badge | +$0.05 per signup (tracked link) |
| Micro | 10–80K | 1 video (Spark/whitelist) + 1 post | $80–250 | +$0.04/signup over 200 |
| Mid | 80–300K | 1 video + 1 X post + Circle co-host | $300–800 | +$0.03/signup over 500 |
| Anchor (1–2 per market) | 300K+ | Launch-week partnership, AMA in their Circle | negotiate | equity-like status: "Nia Partner" badge |

Whitelist/Spark Ads rights on every video (30 days min). Contract = 1 page: usage rights, disclosure (#ad where required), payment on delivery + verified signups.

## 2. Who to pick (per market)

- **Kenya:** campus comedians, gengetone/afro-fusion micro-artists, tech Twitter personalities, football banter pages
- **Nigeria:** skit makers, alté/afrobeets emerging artists, hustle/business content, Nollywood-adjacent film students
- **Ghana:** campus influencers, comedy, fashion/thrift-flip creators
- Cross-market: study-tok/studygram accounts, "opportunities/scholarships" aggregator accounts (perfect fit — they already post what Nia organizes)

Vetting: >2% engagement rate, comments in real local language, no engagement-pod smell, content safe for brand (check last 30 posts).

## 4. Creator brief (1 page, send after yes)

1. **Must say:** what Circles are (30s), that it's free, that it works on any phone
2. **Must show:** real screen-recording — joining a Circle, an opportunities post, a Flick
3. **Hook first 3 seconds** (choose one: group-chat problem / found a gig / algorithm vs Circle)
4. Link in bio + on-screen text with their tracked URL; use #AfricaConnectsHere
5. Disclose partnership per local rules (#ad)
6. Do not: promise payouts to users, show DMs of real people, claim "made in a week" tech myths
7. Deliver: 9:16 master + captions file; we handle Spark whitelisting

## 5. Ambassador program (20 campuses, wave 1–2)

- Recruit 1 per major campus: UoN, Strathmore, KU (KE); UNILAG, Covenant, ABU (NG); UG, KNUST (GH); wave 2: Wits, UCT (ZA), Makerere (UG), UDSM (TZ)
- Kit: violet tee + stickers + roll-up banner + QR standee (install-as-PWA QR)
- Duties: 2 events/term (launch party + Circle workshop), recruit 100 signups/term, run 1 campus Circle
- Track: unique QR/UTM per campus; leaderboard; monthly data stipend on delivery
- Events playbook: 45-min "Find your Circle" workshop → live signup → Circle created for that campus on the spot (empty-room killer)

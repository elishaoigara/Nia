# Nia — Metrics, KPIs & Review Ritual

## 1. North Star

**Weekly Circle Contributors** — members who post, comment, respond ("I can help", "Progress update"), or share an opportunity in a Circle during the week. Not DAU, not minutes: Nia is "building together, not scrolling together," so the number that matters is people *adding* something.

## 2. Funnel KPIs (instrument with consent-aware analytics per repo's release notes)

| Stage | Metric | Launch target | Day-90 target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Acquisition | Signup start rate (visit → form) | 18% | 25% |
| Activation | Signup → onboarding complete | 60% | 75% |
| Activation | Onboarding → joins ≥1 Circle | 55% | 70% |
| Activation | Signup → first post/comment/Flick (≤7d) | 25% | 35% |
| Retention | D1 / D7 / D30 | 40 / 25 / 12% | 50 / 35 / 20% |
| Engagement | WAU/MAU | 30% | 40% |
| Engagement | Weekly Circle Contributors | 3,000 | 10,000 |
| Virality | K-factor (invites accepted/invites sent) | 0.15 | 0.30 |
| Efficiency | Blended CAC | ≤ $0.80 | ≤ $0.50 |
| Efficiency | Paid signup → Circle-join rate | 30% | 40% |
| Safety | Reports / 1k posts — and median time-to-action | < 10 · < 4h | < 8 · < 2h |

## 3. UTM & attribution convention

`utm_source`: meta | tiktok | x | google | youtube | creator | whatsapp | referral | press | producthunt
`utm_medium`: paid | organic | creator | referral | earned
`utm_campaign`: launch | circle-of-week | opportunities-drop | referral-badge | campus-[name]
`utm_content`: [persona]-[angle]-[variant] (e.g., `hustler-opportunities-a1`)
Creators & ambassadors get `?ref=[handle]` links → signup attribution table.

## 4. Dashboards to build (week 0)

1. **Launch war-room (daily, public to team):** signups, onboarding completion, Circle joins, first-contributions, countries, errors, report queue age
2. **Channel scorecard (weekly):** spend, signups, CAC, activation rate by channel — kill/scale decisions live here
3. **Community health (weekly):** per-Circle members/posts/opportunities; dead-Circle alert (no posts 5 days)
4. **Safety board (daily):** reports, actions, appeals, median response time

## 5. Weekly review ritual (Mondays, 45 min)

1. Numbers vs targets table above (10 min — no narratives before numbers)
2. Top/bottom 3 ads and creators — kill or 3× (10 min)
3. Community health: 2 Circles to spotlight, 2 to rescue (10 min)
4. Safety review: every serious incident walked through (5 min)
5. One experiment to ship this week, named owner (10 min)

Monthly: publish transparent numbers to the community (trust is the brand strategy — make it a ritual, not a stunt).

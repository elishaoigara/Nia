# Nia — Press Kit

## 1. Press release (embargo: launch day 07:00 CAT)

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

### Nia launches "Africa Connects Here" — a social platform built around communities, not feeds

**[CITY] — [DATE]** — Nia, a mobile-first social platform for African youth, today opened to the public with a simple thesis: young Africans don't need another endless feed — they need communities that build them up.

Nia is organized around **Circles**: purpose-led communities spanning music, football, technology, business, fashion, education and culture, where members share real opportunities — gigs, scholarships, events — alongside short video (Flicks), stories and direct messaging. The platform runs as a progressive web app that installs from the browser, is engineered for low-bandwidth networks and mid-range Android devices, and offers AI-assisted translation across English, Kiswahili, French, Arabic and Portuguese.

"Social media promised connection and delivered scrolling," said [Founder name], founder of Nia. "'Nia' means intention in Swahili. We built a social network with one: communities where young Africans find their people and their shot. Building together, not scrolling together."

Nia's first 500 founding members — recruited from campus communities and creator circles in Kenya, Nigeria and Ghana — created [N] Circles and shared [N] opportunities during the closed pilot. Safety infrastructure shipped with the product: in-app reporting with appeals, moderator tooling, and member-controlled mutes and blocks.

Nia is free to join at [domain]. Founding members receive a permanent badge and early access to creator tools.

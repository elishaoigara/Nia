## 2. TikTok / IG Reels — video scripts (15–30s, native UGC style)

### Script 1 — "The group chat problem" (Connector, comedy)
- 0–3s HOOK (on-screen text): "POV: you're in 47 WhatsApp groups and still feel lonely 💀"
- 3–12s: fast cuts of chaotic group chats → "So I found Nia — it's like group chats, but organized into Circles: music, football, tech, fashion…"
- 12–22s: screen-record scrolling a Circle, an opportunities post, a Flicks clip
- 22–28s: "Founding members get the badge forever. Link in bio — Africa connects here. ✊🏾"
- CTA overlay: Join free → nia domain

### Script 2 — "I found my shot" (Hustler, testimonial)
- 0–3s: "How I found a paid gig without knowing anybody famous:"
- 3–15s: show joining a Business/Careers Circle → saved opportunity → applied → "they replied same day"
- 15–25s: "Nia Circles are where opportunities get posted before they hit Twitter."
- CTA: "Join free. Link in bio."

### Script 3 — "Algorithm vs Circle" (Creator, opinion piece)
- 0–3s: "TikTok gave me 2M views and zero community. Here's the difference:"
- 3–20s: explain Circles = people who chose to be there; Flicks for your videos; DMs open
- 20–28s: "Bring your crowd. Link in bio." 🟣

### Script 4 — "Data test" (Learner/Hustler, practical)
- 0–3s: "I tested how much data this new African app uses 📶"
- 3–20s: install as PWA from browser, scroll feed, watch a Flick on low-data → show MB used
- 20–27s: "Built for our networks, not Silicon Valley Wi-Fi. Try it."

**Production notes:** film vertically on real phones (not cinema cameras); captions always on (sound-off viewing); first frame must carry the hook as text; shoot 3 variants of each hook; creators film their own versions (Spark Ads whitelist).

## 7. Creative testing plan

- Week 1: test 4 personas × 2 angles × 2 formats = 16 variants, $20–30/day each
- Kill rule: no variant survives 72h at >2× target CPA
- Scale rule: winners get 3× budget; iterate hook-first (first 3s / first line)
- Refresh creatives every 10–14 days (fatigue hits fast at this budget)
- Every ad must show actual product UI at least once
